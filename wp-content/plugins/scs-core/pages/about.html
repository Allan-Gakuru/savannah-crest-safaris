Savannah Crest Safaris was founded by Sam to give visitors a better experience of Africa. We plan journeys around the places you want to see, the people you’re travelling with and how you want to spend your time.

Meet Sam, our founder

Sam has worked as a tour operator in East Africa for 15 years. He started Savannah Crest with a simple mission: to improve every visitor’s experience of Africa, from the first planning conversation to the journey home.

Sam and his team put travellers first at each step. That means listening to what matters to you, explaining the trade-offs and holding the details of your trip to a high standard.

Why travel with us

A route that fits your pace. Tell us how much time you have and what you’d like to see. We’ll help you weigh up an extra park, a longer stay in one place or more time between transfers.

Clear choices before you commit. We talk through travel dates, accommodation, transfers and what’s included, so you can compare options and know what to expect.

Your priorities carry through the trip. If you’re travelling with children, have a particular wildlife interest or need a different pace, bring it up early so we can work it into the route.

How we plan your safari

  1. Tell us what matters. Share a travel window, who’s coming and what you most want to experience.
  2. Review a suggested route. We’ll help you compare options and talk through the pace, stays and costs.
  3. Agree the details. We’ll confirm the itinerary and what’s included before you decide to book.

Safari planning FAQs

Can I adapt one of the listed itineraries?

Yes. Use any itinerary as a starting point. Tell us what you would keep or change and we can discuss routing, dates and available accommodation.

What affects the cost of a safari?

Season, group size, accommodation, transport and park fees can all affect the cost. The trip page explains its listed price basis. We’ll confirm the cost and inclusions for your dates as part of planning.

Can I enquire before choosing dates or a destination?

Yes. Share the length of trip you’re considering, who’s travelling and what you’re looking forward to. That’s enough to start a conversation.
