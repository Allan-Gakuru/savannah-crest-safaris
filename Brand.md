# Savannah Crest Safaris

The Savannah Crest Safaris identity is built around the pointed shield monogram. Its two open sides and diagonal centre stroke are the details that make the shield recognizable. Use the full logo where the brand name needs to be read and the shield alone where space is limited.

## Brand colors

These hex values are the master palette for digital artwork.

| Color | Hex | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Espresso | `#2B1A14` | `43, 26, 20` | Primary shield and wordmark, body text, dark backgrounds |
| Light Cream | `#F7F1E5` | `247, 241, 229` | Primary background and reversed shield and wordmark |
| Antique Gold | `#B08A48` | `176, 138, 72` | The **SAFARIS** line in the full-color logo and small decorative accents |

Use Espresso and Light Cream for reading and navigation. Antique Gold against Light Cream has a contrast ratio of **2.84:1**, so reserve that combination for the approved logo and decorative details.

## Logo files

The master artwork is in [assets/brand/svg](assets/brand/svg). The matching [PNG exports](assets/brand/png) have transparent backgrounds, with an Espresso background on the social avatar. The wordmark is outlined, so the SVGs render without fonts.

| Artwork | Asset filename stem | Where to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary stacked | `logo-primary` | Default on Light Cream and other light, uncluttered backgrounds |
| Reversed stacked | `logo-reverse` | On Espresso and other dark, uncluttered backgrounds |
| Horizontal | `logo-horizontal` | Website headers and other wide spaces on light backgrounds |
| Reversed horizontal | `logo-horizontal-reverse` | Wide spaces on dark backgrounds |
| One-color stacked | `logo-mono-espresso`, `logo-mono-cream` | Stamps, engraving, embroidery, or single-color printing |
| One-color horizontal | `logo-horizontal-mono-espresso`, `logo-horizontal-mono-cream` | Wide applications with a single ink or material |
| Standalone shield | `shield-espresso`, `shield-cream` | Small placements, watermarks, and secondary marks |
| Social avatar | `social-avatar` | Profile photos with square or circular crops. Upload the 1080 × 1080 PNG. |

[Website icons](assets/brand/web) include `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` with 16, 32, and 48 px sizes, `favicon-32.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` at 180 × 180 px.

## Using the mark

- Keep at least one shield-stroke width of clear space around the complete visible mark. Leave more room when the logo is placed near a headline or another brand.
- As a starting point for screen layouts, use the stacked logo at 160 px wide or more, and the horizontal logo at 240 px wide or more. Use the shield on its own below those widths. At favicon sizes, use the files supplied specifically for that purpose.
- Use the supplied proportions and wordmark spacing. Apply the one-color variant when the process calls for a single ink or material.
