Field notes from Savannah Crest Safaris, with ideas and practical questions for planning your own trip.

What would you do with another morning in the Mara?

If the Maasai Mara is the reason you’re coming to Kenya, look at how many full days each route gives you in the reserve. An arrival afternoon, a full day and a departure morning give you different windows for game drives. Ask which days are mainly for transfers before comparing two itineraries of the same length.

Give Amboseli time to unfold

Think about what you want time for: watching elephants, photographing the changing light or spending a quiet afternoon at your camp. If the Kilimanjaro backdrop is high on your list, leave room for more than one outing. Clouds can change the view over the course of a day.

Adding the Rift Valley lakes to a Mara safari

Our catalogue includes itineraries that pair the Mara with Lake Nakuru or add Lake Naivasha. Compare which activities are included at each stop, where you’ll spend the night and how much of each day is set aside for driving. Those details tell you more about the pace than the number of stops on the itinerary.
